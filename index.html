Our Legacy

Founded in 1980 by the Government of Rwanda in partnership with the Diocese of Butare, GS Gasagara has evolved into a beacon of academic and moral excellence. Our commitment to holistic education integrates STEM, arts, and Christian values inspired by St. Gabriel. We serve over 800 students from nursery to ordinary level, with boarding facilities and cutting-edge labs.

🌟 Vision: To be a continental model school producing innovative, responsible Rwandan leaders.
📖 Mission: Affordable, quality education fostering critical thinking, discipline, and community service.

School History & Achievements

GS Gasagara started with 45 students in O’Level. Today we board over 300 students in 7 dormitories. Recognized by Gisagara District for excellence in sciences and discipline (2020, 2024). Motto "Discipline – Travail – Réussite" reflects our core values.
