About GS GASAGARA

Geographical Location: Southern Province, Gisagara District, Gikonko Sector.

Historical Background

GS Gasagara is a government school that has evolved to serve its community by providing continuous basic education. It has grown from its foundational years to encompass Nursery, Primary, and Secondary levels, reflecting a commitment to accessible education for all in the sector. The school's development aligns with national efforts to expand educational infrastructure and inclusivity.

Our Mission

To provide quality, inclusive education that develops responsible citizens equipped with knowledge, skills, and values for lifelong learning and community contribution.

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence in Gisagara District, recognized for holistic student development and commitment to accessible education for all.

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive education from early childhood through secondary level (O'Level)

Nursery Level

Early childhood education focusing on foundational skills, social development, and school readiness. Total: 418 students (201 boys, 217 girls).

Primary Level

Core curriculum including Mathematics, Science, Languages (Kinyarwanda, English, French), Social Studies, and character formation. Total: 1,397 students (703 boys, 694 girls).

Secondary Level (O'Level)

Comprehensive lower secondary education preparing students for national examinations and further studies. Total: 918 students (415 boys, 503 girls).
