Redefining Education

Since 2015, we've shaped global citizens with academic rigor, cutting-edge technology, and unwavering character.

AI & Robotics Hub

First school in East Africa with dedicated AI lab, machine learning bootcamps, and IBO-sponsored robotics competitions. Students code, innovate, compete globally.

Global Partnerships

Exchange programs with Cambridge, Stanford, and African leaders. Model UN delegations, international debating, cross-cultural leadership retreats.

Eco-Campus

100% solar-powered. Carbon-neutral operations. Organic gardens, water harvesting, sustainability embedded in all curricula. Green thinking from Day 1.

Wellness First

State-of-the-art sports complex, mental health counselors, yoga studios, and nutritionist-planned meals. Balanced excellence.

Multilingual Excellence

English (medium), French, Kinyarwanda, Mandarin, Arabic. Language exchange immersion programs in partnership with leading international schools.

Entrepreneurship Hub

Student-led startups, venture pitch competitions, business incubation—turning ideas into ventures. Real-world impact starting at age 12.

Academic Excellence

Cambridge IGCSE + Rwanda National Curriculum = Global Ready

STEM Mastery

Advanced Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics with laboratory research. Math Olympiad coaching, Science fairs, and hands-on projects with real datasets.

Humanities & Arts

English Literature, History, Geography, Philosophy. Critical thinking, debate championships, cultural immersion weeks across Africa and beyond.

Computer Science

Python, JavaScript, Web Development, AI/ML fundamentals. Coding bootcamps, hackathons, app development with real-world mentors from tech hubs.
