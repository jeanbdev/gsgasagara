About Us

Founded in 1980, Groupe Scolaire Gasagara is a school governed by the government of Rwanda and has established itself as a center of academic excellence in the Huye-Gikonko region.

We are committed to providing quality education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and social responsibility. Our dedicated staff and modern facilities create the perfect environment for your child's success.

Our Vision: To be a leading school in Rwanda that produces learned, responsible citizens with strong character and academic excellence.

Our Mission: To provide affordable, quality education that develops critical thinking, moral values, and practical skills for all levels.

Our Motto: Discipline – Travail – Réussite (Discipline – Work – Success)

Amateka y’Ishuri / School History

Groupe Scolaire Gasagara yashinzwe mu 1980. Ryatangijwe n'Letarwanda ifatanije na Diyoseze ya Butare. Yatangiye ikiciro rusange (O'Level) hamwe n'abanyeshuri 45 gusa. Mu myaka yakurikiyeho, ishuri ryagutse ryongeramo amashuri abanza (primary) n'ay'incuke (nursery).

Kuva ryatangira, ryamenyekanye cyane mu kwigisha siyanse n'ikoranabuhanga, ndetse no mu iterambere ry'imyitwarire myiza. Muri 2020, ryahawe ibihembo n'akarere ka Gisagara kubera ibisubizo byiza mu bizamini bya Leta.

Uyu munsi, GS Gasagara ifite abanyeshuri barenga 800, abarimu 35 n'abakozi 12 bafasha. Ikomeje guteza imbere ubumenyi ndetse n'indangagaciro za Gikirisitu zishingiye ku murimo wa Mutagatifu Gabriel.

"Discipline – Travail – Réussite" niwo mwirongo wacu uyobora imibereho myiza y'ishuri.
